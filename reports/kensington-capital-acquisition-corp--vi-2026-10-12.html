Kensington Capital Acquisition Corp. VI is structured as a SPAC, which is fundamentally different from a thrift conversion (mutual-to-stock IPO). SPACs do not have loan portfolios, deposit bases, net interest margins, or capital ratios in the traditional banking sense prior to completing an acquisition. The ticker suffixes (-UN, -WT) are characteristic of SPAC unit and warrant structures, further confirming this is not a depository institution IPO. Without a target acquisition identified and no prospectus text provided, none of the 10 checklist criteria applicable to community bank analysis can be evaluated.
