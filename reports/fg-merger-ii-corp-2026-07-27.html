FG Merger II Corp. Without a target acquisition identified and closed, there is no underlying bank balance sheet to evaluate against the 10-point thrift conversion checklist. Investors considering this vehicle should understand they are buying into a blind pool with no guaranteed banking target, which carries materially different risk characteristics than a traditional thrift conversion. This submission cannot be meaningfully scored under the thrift conversion framework.
